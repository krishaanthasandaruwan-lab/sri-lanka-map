This is not Google Maps data. Coverage depends on OpenStreetMap: missing buildings stay missing. Roof colours, forest texture and road markings are illustrative, not surveyed. Footprints and road paths come from the map data.

Choose Front, Right, Back or Left to see the whole map from four bird’s-eye directions. Unity Plaza and Majestic City have a separate 2D illustration for each view. Rear and unseen side details are approximate artistic interpretations; the artwork has finite image resolution.

Everything needed to view this map is in this folder, including the country archive. No account or internet connection is needed.
